Data availability. The processed building exposure inventory (585,033 records), district-level loss panels, index series and all figure source data generated in this study are available at [Zenodo DOI to be assigned]. District boundaries follow the official 2024 county-level division product (Tianditu, map review no. GS(2024)0650); the boundary vector is not redistributed. Building footprints and their attributes were derived from CMAB (Zhang et al., 2025; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04730-5). Source data: IBTrACS v4r01 from NOAA NCEI (https://doi.org/10.25921/82ty-9e16); FABDEM V1-2 from the University of Bristol data repository (Hawker et al., 2022); LandScan population grids from Oak Ridge National Laboratory (https://landscan.ornl.gov; https://doi.org/10.48690/1524213); ETOPO 2022 from NOAA NCEI (https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74), from which the shelf-geometry parameters (550 km width, 55 m mean depth) were derived; gridded near-surface wind speed from the China Meteorological Forcing Dataset v2.0 (He et al., 2024; National Tibetan Plateau Data Center); precipitation from CHM_PRE V2 (Hu and Miao, 2025; https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14634575); altimeter sea-level anomalies from the Copernicus Marine Service (https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00148); annual impervious-surface-ratio rasters (1985–2020) derived from GAIA (Gong et al., 2020); district-level demographic indicators compiled from China's 5th, 6th and 7th national population censuses; monthly gridded minimum and maximum air temperature (0.05°), interpolated by inverse-distance weighting from NOAA NCEI Global Surface Summary of the Day station records; reported annual ocean-disaster loss series compiled from the China Marine Disaster Bulletins (Ministry of Natural Resources; 1995–2023). Three source groups are not redistributed here: the gridded meteorological, sea-level, impervious-surface and LandScan population rasters; the monthly second-hand housing price series, obtained from Juhui Data (https://www.gotohui.com) under a commercial data subscription; and the community-level listings, obtained from Anjuke. The shoreline geometry used by the surge model was taken from a third-party layer distributed by 立方数据学社 (Lifang Data Academy), whose upstream provenance could not be established; that layer supplies only the shoreline polyline (the land-sea mask is derived from the FABDEM terrain) and is not redistributed. We independently verified that layer against official reference data before use, and its Shanghai outline is consistent with the official division product.
